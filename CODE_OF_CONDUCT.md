# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Summary

Be respectful and constructive. Criticism of ideas is welcome; personal attacks are not. The goal is a productive, inclusive environment for everyone.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to **andreas.burner@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 is available at:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
